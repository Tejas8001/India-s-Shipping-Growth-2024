* India's Shipping Growth 2024

A Power BI dashboard that looks at how India's merchant shipping fleet has grown over the years, comparing coastal and overseas vessels.
** Dataset
Taken from the Government of India open data portal: [https://www.data.gov.in/resource/growth-indian-shipping-1947-2024]

It has yearly data on the number of vessels and Gross Registered Tonnage (GRT, the total internal volume of a ship) for coastal and overseas shipping.
  DASHBOARD - https://github.com/Tejas8001/India-s-Shipping-Growth-2024/blob/main/Shipping%20Dashboard.png
 
** What the dashboard shows
- Total GRT, total vessels, average GRT and number of years covered
- Coastal vs overseas share of vessels and average GRT
- Year-wise trend of GRT
- Year-wise trend of average vessel size

** Findings
- Total GRT has grown steadily, mostly because of overseas shipping.
- Coastal GRT has stayed almost flat.
- Overseas vessels are much bigger on average than coastal ones.
- Average coastal vessel size has dropped over the years.
